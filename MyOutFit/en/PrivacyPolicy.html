GlamShow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 27, 2026

GlamShow (hereinafter referred to as "this App" or "we") understands the importance of privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information, especially when you use the AI virtual try-on feature.

Please read and understand this Privacy Policy carefully before using this App. By starting to use this App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide core virtual try-on services, we need access to the following information:

  • Camera Permission: Used to take a selfie or environment photo to acquire an image for virtual try-on.
  • Photo Library Permission: Allows you to select photos from your local album for virtual try-on and to save the generated results.
  • Face Processing: User-uploaded photos are only used for cloud-based AI try-on synthesis. We do not perform identity recognition, nor do we store your original biometric face data.

2. How Information is Used

  • AI Try-on Synthesis: When you initiate a try-on task, the face image you select or capture is uploaded to our cloud server for AI try-on synthesis.
  • Temporary Storage and Processing: Uploaded images are only used for the current try-on task. Once the task is completed, the generated image is returned to your App.

3. Information Security and Protection

  • No Account System: This App does not have a registration or login process; your personal identity information (such as phone number, email, social accounts, etc.) will not be collected by us.
  • Localized Storage: All your try-on records, task statuses, and generated results are stored locally on your device (Isar/SQLite database).
  • Encrypted Transmission: We use secure signature protocols (SHA256) to protect communication with the server.

4. Your Rights

  • Delete Data: You can click "Clear All Local Data" in the App settings or "My Gallery" at any time to completely erase all your traces on the device.
  • Withdraw Authorization: You can withdraw access authorization for the camera and photo library at any time through your device system settings.

5. Third-Party Services

This App may integrate the following third-party SDKs to provide necessary features:

  • Flutter Official Libraries and Plugins: Used to build the core App functionality.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via:
